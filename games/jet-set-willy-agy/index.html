JET SET WILLY - HOW TO PLAY

The Story

Miner Willy struck it rich in Manic Miner and purchased a colossal 60-room mansion. After an all-night party, Maria the housekeeper will not let Willy go to sleep in his Master Bedroom until every flashing item (82 in total) is collected from around the estate!

Original Mechanics

  • Fixed Jump Trajectory: Willy's jumps follow a fixed parabolic arc. You cannot change direction in mid-air!
  • Slopes & Ramps: Walk smoothly up and down diagonal slopes.
  • Conveyors: Belts pull Willy in the direction they roll. Walk with them to speed up, or fight against them.
  • Ropes: In The Quirkafleeg, On the Roof, Cold Store, Swimming Pool, and The Beach, grab swinging ropes, ride across chasms, and jump off!
  • Fall Height: Falling too far (more than 4-5 blocks) is fatal!
  • Sleepwalker Ending: Once all 82 items are collected, head to the Master Bedroom, jump in bed, and watch Willy sleepwalk into the bathroom for the final toilet flush!

Classic Cheat Code

Type writetyper at any time to enable the classic cheat mode! You can also use the Mansion Room Warp selector at the bottom to explore any of the 60 rooms.
