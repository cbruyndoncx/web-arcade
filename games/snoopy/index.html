HOW TO PLAY • SNOOPY TABLETOP

1

Protect Sleeping Woodstock!

At the bottom, Schroeder plays his red toy piano, sending a cascade of musical notes floating upwards towards Woodstock's nest at the top right. If any note reaches the nest, Woodstock wakes up in distress and you get a MISS (3 misses = Game Over).

2

Fluid Movement Across 4 Platforms

Press ← → (or A D / Red Joystick) to guide Snoopy across the 4 colored platform posts. Snoopy now glides smoothly from post to post.

1: RED 2: GREEN 3: BLUE 4: YELLOW
3

Smash Notes in the Strike Zone

Each platform has a glowing circular Strike Target. When a rising note enters the circle over your current platform, press SPACE, ↑, or the big red SMASH button!

  • 🎯 Normal Hit: +1 Point
  • ⭐ Dead-Center Perfect Hit: +3 Points & double score bonus!
4

Modes & Special Events

Game A: 3 note tracks at a relaxed tempo • Game B: 4 note tracks at high speed!

Every 100 Points: Lucy storms in and kicks Schroeder's piano! At 300 Points, all your misses are completely wiped clean!
